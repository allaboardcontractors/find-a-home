October 2026: more choices, slower sales

The Greater Houston housing market slowed down in October, and buyers have more homes to choose from than they have had in a long time. Sellers are adjusting to that change. Here are the numbers from the Houston Association of Realtors, released November 11, 2026.

6,348

Single family homes sold in October, down 14.9% from last October. The fewest for any October in 10 years.

$320,000

Median single family price, down 3.0% from last year. The lowest since January 2024.

$421,100

Average single family price, about the same as last October.

40,253

Homes for sale, up 6.8% from last October.

5.5 months

Months of inventory, the most since 2012.

59 days

Average days on the market, the same as last October.

Across all property types, 7,485 properties sold in October, down 15.1% from a year earlier. Pending sales fell 17.6% to 6,445, and pending sales usually turn into next month closings, so November is likely to stay slow too.

Existing homes, not new construction, told the same story: sales down 10.2%, average price $428,790, median price $315,000, down 4.5%. Townhomes and condominiums had even more supply, 9.4 months, and took 65 days to sell.

Source: Houston Association of Realtors, October 2026 Housing Market Update, released November 11, 2026. Read the HAR update.

What it means for buyers

You have more choice and more time than you had a year ago, and the median price is lower. That does not mean every home is a deal. Homes that are priced right and in good shape still move, and homes that are priced too high sit. Get your budget and your must haves clear first, then look at homes that fit, not just homes that look cheap. If you are buying with owner financing, tell me that up front, it changes which homes I show you.

What it means for sellers

Pricing it right the first time matters more now. With 5.5 months of inventory, buyers can walk away and pick another home. A home that sits for 59 days or more starts to look stale, and that costs you money. If you are thinking about selling, ask me what homes like yours actually sold for in your neighborhood, not what you hope it will sell for.
